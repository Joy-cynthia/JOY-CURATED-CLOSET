Who We Are

Founded by Joy Cynthia Wachera, the Joy Cynthia Foundation bridges the gap between potential and opportunity.We equip young women and youth with practical digital skills, financial independence tools and psychological first aid advocacy so they can rewrite their futures with confidence.

Core Community Programs

Digital Skills & Remote Work

Providing sanitary essentials, reproductive health education and confidence workshop for primary and high school girls.

Mental Health & PFA Support

Promoting psychological first aid awareness, emotional resilience and safe peer support spaces for youth.

Community Welfare Drives

Organizing food drives, clothing donations and emergency family support in local neighbourhoods
